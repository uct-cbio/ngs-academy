Competencies Mapper

Map content against the Pathogen Genomics Competency Framework

How to use: Paste a curriculum, course outline, job description, CV, training record, or self-assessment — or drop a .txt, .md, .pdf, or .docx file onto the box below — then click Evaluate. The engine scans for PGCF topic keywords and Bloom's taxonomy action verbs in the same sentence to map both coverage and cognitive level. Tip: press Ctrl+Enter (or ⌘+Enter) inside the box to evaluate.

🚫 Coverage Gaps

📐 Depth Profile

For topics that are covered, this shows the highest cognitive level at which they appear — based on Bloom's taxonomy verbs found in the same sentence as the topic keywords. Not gaps, but useful for understanding the depth of treatment.
